Vision & Expertise

Professional Interests
& Technical Foundations

My engineering philosophy is simple: rigorous mathematical understanding under the hood, clean reproducible software architecture, and building systems that hold up under high-stakes, real-world deployment constraints.

⚡

Edge AI & Model Optimization

Translating large deep learning models onto resource-constrained embedded targets (such as the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano). I focus on kernel fusion (Conv-BN), surgical weight modifications (channel slicing), mixed-precision execution (FP16), and compilation pipelines with ONNX and TensorRT to maximize throughput while minimizing power and latency.

🏥

Healthcare & Assistive AI

Building AI for domains where accuracy and reliability directly impact human lives. From diabetic retinopathy severity classification and 3D cell tracking in live microscopy to real-time multimodal vision-language assistants for the visually impaired, I am passionate about assistive and clinical technologies engineered with rigorous error margins.

🔍

Production Deployment Reliability

Bridging the critical gap between offline evaluation metrics and live closed-loop performance. My research investigates why models that pass per-frame tolerance testing can still fail catastrophically in deployment due to precision shifts, argmax peak-flips, and compounding error drift across sequential frames.

🤖

Multimodal Foundation Models

Adapting and fine-tuning open-source vision-language architectures (such as Qwen2-VL) for domain-specific applications. I utilize parameter-efficient fine-tuning (LoRA/PEFT) combined with inference-stage acceleration (SDPA kernel fusion, KV caching, and patch boundary alignment) to make foundation models production-ready.
